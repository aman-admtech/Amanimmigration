Document Preparation

Essential Document Checklist

Wondering what papers to prepare before visiting us? Select your visa category to see the required documentation.

Valid Passport

Original passport with at least 6 months validity from date of travel.

Academic Marksheets

10th, 12th, and Degree/Diploma DMC certificates (original & photocopies).

IELTS / PTE / Medium of Instruction

English test scorecard OR MOI certificate for Without-IELTS countries.

Financial & Bank Proofs

Sponsor's bank statements, ITR records, or education loan letter.

Invitation Letter

Formal invitation letter from host family member or friend residing abroad.

Host's Legal Status Proof

Host's PR card, work permit, or citizenship passport copy.

Applicant Financials

Last 6 months bank statement showing adequate funds and source of income.

Ties to Home Country

Property evaluation, agricultural land proof, or employment leave letter.

Travel Itinerary

Day-by-day travel plan, planned sight-seeing list, and tentative flight schedule.

Hotel Accommodations

Confirmed hotel reservation vouchers covering full travel duration.

Income Tax Returns (ITR)

Last 2 to 3 years personal or business Income Tax Return acknowledgments.

Photographs (Country Spec)

Recent white-background biometric passport photographs matching embassy rules.

Marriage Certificate

Government registered marriage certificate issued by Registrar of Marriages.

Relationship Proofs

Wedding photographs, marriage invitations, joint travel records & call logs.

Primary Spouse's Status

Valid student study permit or work permit, college enrollment letter & payslips.

Police Clearance & Medical

Valid PCC certificate from Regional Passport Office and approved medical tests.
