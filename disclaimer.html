1. Sole Jurisdiction of Embassies & Consulates

Every visa application (including Study Visa, Visitor Visa, Tourist Visa, Spouse / Dependent Visa, and Work Permit) is independently evaluated and decided by immigration officers authorized by the sovereign government of the destination country:

  • Canada: Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)
  • Australia: Department of Home Affairs (DHA)
  • United Kingdom: UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI)
  • European Union / Schengen: Respective Embassy Consulates & VFS Global
  • Singapore: Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA)
  • United Arab Emirates (Dubai): General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA)
  • Russia & Belarus: Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Consular Sections

No Visa Guarantee: Aman Immigration has no influence, connection, or authority over the decisions made by visa officers.
